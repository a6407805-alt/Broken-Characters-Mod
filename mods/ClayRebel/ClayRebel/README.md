# Clay Rebel

A **blue traitor Clay Templar** who turned on the Dunestrider. Tar your enemies, then blow them up.

| Slot | Skill | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Passive | **Born of Clay** | Tar puddles don't slow you. |
| Primary | **Tar Minigun** | Spin up and fire about **15 bullets/s** at **25%** each. Every bullet has a **full chance to trigger items**. |
| Secondary | **Tar Blast** | A cone shockwave for **300%** that knocks enemies back and covers them in **Tar** for 6s (slowed). 5s cooldown. |
| Utility | **Clay C4** | Throw a sticky bomb that clings to enemies or terrain, then explodes for **1000%** and **ignites**. **Tarred enemies take double damage.** 8s cooldown. |
| Special | **Summon Rebels** | Call **5 blue Clay Templars** to fight for you for **2 minutes**. 2m 15s cooldown. |

Half the size of a normal Clay Templar (so are his Rebels). Stats: 200 HP (+60/level), 20 armor, 12 damage (+2.4/level). Every number is configurable in
`BepInEx/config/com.VincentMacaluso.ClayRebel.cfg`.

Everyone in a multiplayer lobby needs the mod.
