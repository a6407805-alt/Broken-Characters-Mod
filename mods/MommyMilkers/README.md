# Mommy Milkers

A tall, sunny beach girl in a red bikini with a gallon of milk and a brand-new car from her parents.

**Personal use only.** The van wears Honda's "H" and the bottles are Gatorade: play it with friends, don't upload it to Thunderstore.

## Milk in the Tank (passive)

- Milking uses her **tank** (100 milk, 25 a second): the jug gauge left of the crosshair shows it.
- It refills by itself (8 a second, after 1 s without milking).
- Run dry and the jug sputters: she needs a quarter tank before it sprays again.

## Milking (primary, hold)

- She holds the jug up at her chest and squeezes: a stream of milk out to 25 m.
- Goes through every enemy in the stream: 90% damage 5 times a second (faster with attack speed), knocks them back.

## Reload (secondary, 8 s)

- **Hold:** chugs a Gatorade. The tank fills right up and lightning arcs off her into enemies within 18 m (100% per bolt, chains to 3 more).
- **Tap:** throws a full Gatorade. Where it splits, the spill shocks up to 4 enemies within 9 m (80%) every 0.4 s for 5 s.

## Side to Side (utility, 15 s after the ride)

- She rides her green beach cruiser, waving and cheering.
- **Mash Shift to pedal faster.** Stop pedalling and it slows down; below walking pace she gets off.
- Steer by looking. +80 armor, no attacks while riding.
- Allies within 30 m get **+35% move speed and attack speed** while she cheers them on.

## Cool Car (special, 28 s)

- A selfie: "Wow, my parents got me a new car! It's soooo cool!"
- Her grey minivan pulls up and honks... then explodes for **5000% damage** in 25 m. She cries.
- She can't be hurt while it plays (about 10 s).

## Install

- Put the `MommyMilkers` folder in `BepInEx/plugins` (r2modman: import the zip as a local mod).
- Her model and props need `mommymilkersbundle` next to `MommyMilkers.dll` (built in Unity from the `-Unity.zip`).
  Without it she's a tinted Commando and the props are boxes.
- Every player needs the mod. Settings: `BepInEx/config/com.VincentMacaluso.MommyMilkers.cfg` (one Volume, default 75).
