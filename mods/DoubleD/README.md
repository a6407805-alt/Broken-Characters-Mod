# The Real DoubleD

Seven feet of handles, hair gel and trash talk: a G-Eazy look-alike (dark-blonde slicked-back hair) in a Miami Heat #3
jersey. Three skins: Home, Away, Miami Vice. Personal use only (a real person's likeness, NBA/Heat marks, Toyota).

## Dribble (passive) + Full Court Press (sprint)

- He's always dribbling, and the dribble changes at random (crossover, between the legs, behind the back, pound).
- While he dribbles, enemy hits have a **65%** chance to **bounce off the ball**: blocked and sent back at the attacker.
  Every bounce switches the dribble.
- **Full Court Press** (sprint): no bounces, but **+45 armor**, 1.8x sprint speed, and **350%** to everyone he runs through.

## Murder Him, Murder Him (primary, 3 s)

- A hoop drops in front of him and he throws down a random dunk (tomahawk, windmill, 360, between the legs):
  **670%** to everything within 22 m of him. Then he hangs on the rim: "Stop fouling me!"

## They Say I Look Like G-Easy (secondary, 8 s)

- He combs his hair back: a **full barrier**, and lightning strikes every enemy within 50 m for **369%**.

## Mommas Boy (utility, 10 s)

- "I'm not playing anymore!" A yawn, and he flops into Mom's freshly made bed. **5 s** asleep: heals to full but takes
  **+50%** damage. A dream bubble (a goldfish in a bowl). "Thanks Mom!"
- **True Trauma** (2% per nap): the dream comes true. A giant goldfish with X eyes, **5,000,000 health** that never
  scales. Only he can hurt it and it only hunts him: slow, but its light beams hit hard, and his world goes dark and
  foggy while it lives. Kill it and he becomes **Dirk Divine** for the rest of the run: he glows, -50% cooldowns,
  +100% damage, +100% health, +10 regen, +50 armor.

## Taking Strikes Like a Bowler! (special, 25 s)

- A swipe grabs up to **3** enemies in front of him (bosses too) and takes them to a **pocket dimension** high above
  the map. They're stood up as pins at the end of a cosmic bowling lane while he raps ("People say I look like G-Eazy... I'm taking strikes like a bowler!"), then he bowls,
  the camera rides the ball down the lane, **9999%**. STRIKE!

## Stop at the Yellow, Go Through the Red (alt special, 20 s)

- His white Camry at a red light in the pocket dimension, cars behind. The light flips yellow and green... press
  **Primary within 1 s of it turning red** (a big traffic light shows at the top of the screen): he floors it,
  **4500% damage to every enemy on the map**.
  Too early or too late: the cars honk, the Camry blows up, and he's stuck at **1 health for 20 s**.

All numbers are in the config (`BepInEx/config/com.VincentMacaluso.DoubleD.cfg`); set "True Trauma Chance" to 1 to
meet the Goldfish on the next nap.

## Install

- Put the `DoubleD` folder in `BepInEx/plugins` (r2modman: import the zip as a local mod). Needs R2API Items too.
- His model and props need `doubledbundle` next to `DoubleD.dll`, built in Unity from the `-Unity.zip`.
