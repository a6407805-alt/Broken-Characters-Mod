# Brother

*"Brother, may I have some oats?"*

The pig from the legendary video, escaped from the farm: a slow, huge tank who gets stronger with every bowl of oats.
Custom model, animations, three skins (Default, Berkshire, Mud Wallow), synthesized voice lines and sounds.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Oats** (passive) | Every 20 kills = 1 Oats stack (up to 50): +2% movement speed, +4% damage, +5% health, +1 armor each. Every 10 Oats, **the Farmer** comes. |
| **Truffle Shake** | A quick breakdance: flips on his back and spins for 1.6 s, 120% to everything within 7 m every 0.3 s, with a little truffle song. 2 s cooldown. |
| **Beckon** | A sonic OINK: a 20 m beam through every enemy in the line, 600%, stuns and knocks back. 5 s. |
| **Roll** | Curl up and roll for 2 s, steering with movement: 350% to enemies you bowl through, allies knocked aside, +100 armor. 6 s. |
| **Slam** | Blast off like a rocket, then steer a predator-missile view (WASD, Primary/Jump to boost) to pick where you land: 15,000% in 35 m, **teammates included**. Leaves radiation on every enemy on the map and a 60 m fallout zone. 90 s. |

## The Farmer

A 15 m farmer with a double-barrelled shotgun, hunting Brother. He comes every 10 Oats (phase 1 at 10, up to phase 5 at 50),
yells "Yeeeeehaaaaaw!!", gets a boss health bar, and sends cryptic messages in chat between visits.

| Phase | Oats | New attacks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | Shotgun blast; grabs players and throws them or slams them into the ground |
| 2 | 20 | Corn stalks burst out of the ground under the players |
| 3 | 30 | Buckshot rain from the sky |
| 4 | 40 | Thrown hay bales, both barrels, red eyes |
| 5 | 50 | Bullet hell: spinning rings of glowing buckshot |

Beat him at phase 1-4 and he retreats until the next 10. Beat him at **phase 5** and every Brother gets **Freedom** (his gold
shotgun: x10 all stats) and every other player gets a random legendary item.

Every number is in `BepInEx/config/com.VincentMacaluso.OatsBrother.cfg`. Custom sounds play through Windows directly (one
"Volume" setting, 75 by default).
