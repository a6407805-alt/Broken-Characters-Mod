Optional custom sound effects for Berserk Fury (this PC only).
Name the files exactly like this (.wav or .mp3) and they replace the vanilla sounds:
  m1.wav          Twin Buster Blaster shot
  drill.wav       Magneser Drill
  boost.wav       Ion Thruster Surge
  cpc_charge.wav  Charged Particle Cannon charge-up
  cpc_fire.wav    Charged Particle Cannon blast
  overdrive.wav   Particle Energy hits 100%
  comm.wav        pilot comm window opens
Don't commit ripped game audio to this public repo; keep it on your PC or in the private Extras repo.
