Berserk Fury pilot comm window
==============================
Drop your own voice clips here (.wav or .mp3), one folder per moment.
A random clip from the folder plays; without clips the Windows voice reads the line through a radio filter.

  launch\     run start / respawn
  cpc\        Charged Particle Cannon (R)
  drill\      Magneser Drill (M2)
  boost\      Ion Thruster Surge (Shift)
  overdrive\  Particle Energy hits 100%
  hurt\       health drops under 30%
  kill\       killing an elite
  boss\       killing a boss
  levelup\    level up
  battle\     teleporter starts      tpdone\  teleporter charged
  streak\     12 hits in 2.5 s       bighit\  a big hit taken
  idle\       banter mid-fight       ally\    teammate died
  death\      you die                revive\  you come back

command.png (square) replaces the generated COMMAND operator portrait.

portrait.png (square) replaces the generated pilot silhouette.
