# Berserk Fury (Zoids: Battle Legends)

The ultimate Tyrannosaur Zoid and rival to Liger Zero, ported straight from GameCube's *Zoids: Battle Legends*!

### Abilities
- **Passive: Organoid Drive & E-Shield** — Building Particle Reserves empowers abilities. Sprinting and charging abilities activates a frontal **E-Shield** granting bonus armor and projectile deflection.
- **Primary (M1): Twin Buster Blaster** — Fires alternating high-velocity particle beam rounds from the back-mounted Buster Claws (320% damage). Melee strikes inflict bleeding.
- **Secondary (M2): Magneser Drill Thrust** — Rapidly spins both Buster Claws into piercing drills, impaling and shredding enemies (7x80% damage) with armor reduction.
- **Utility (Shift): Ion Thruster Surge** — Ignites high-output ion thrusters to jet forward with immense velocity, leaving an ion trail and knocking foes aside.
- **Special (R): Charged Particle Cannon (CPC)** — Deploys ground anchors and tail cooling vents, charging a radiant particle vortex before discharging a devastating continuous mega-beam (2400% damage) that pierces across the entire map.
