# Sir Reginald, the Pigeon Knight

A plump city pigeon in plate armour, sworn to defend the bread of the realm. Kettle helm with a red plume, a baguette
lance with a pie-tin guard, a bottle-cap shield.

## Flock (passive)

- Pigeons gather around him while an enemy is near (one every **3 s**) and **one per kill**, up to **6**.
- Each pigeon pecks the nearest enemy within 20 m for **35%** damage every second.

## Baguette Lance (primary)

- Hold for the combo: poke **180%**, poke **180%**, spinning lunge **300%** with knockback.

## Coo of Challenge (secondary, 8 s)

- A mighty COO: every enemy within **30 m targets him, bosses included**, for 4 s. He gets **+60 armor** and the flock
  pecks **twice as fast**.

## Flap (utility, 3 charges)

- A short, strong flap up and forward. **Keep holding to glide.**

## Statue Day (special, 16 s)

- He raises the lance: **hundreds of pigeons dive-bomb** the spot he aims at (12 m, **8 x 150%**). A **giant statue of
  him** rises there and he swoops onto it. For 10 s, near his statue he deals **+50% damage** and his flock stays full.

All numbers are in the config (`BepInEx/config/com.VincentMacaluso.PigeonKnight.cfg`).

## Install

- Put the `PigeonKnight` folder in `BepInEx/plugins` (r2modman: import the zip as a local mod).
- His model and props need `pigeonknightbundle` next to `PigeonKnight.dll`, built in Unity from the `-Unity.zip`.
